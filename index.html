Help & FAQ

A complete guide to every section, alerts, analytics, privacy and troubleshooting.

Getting started

Sign in or create an account. Create your workspace using your business name. Your workspace keeps your leads and activity together. Use the left menu to move between sections.

Overview & dashboard

Overview shows total leads, new opportunities, follow-ups and won clients. The quick cards open filtered leads so you can focus on what needs attention.

Adding and editing leads

Choose + New lead, enter the business, contact details, source, pipeline stage, follow-up date and notes, then Save changes. Open any lead card to edit it. Delete removes a lead after confirmation; this is different from archiving an incoming request.

Pipeline stages

New means not yet contacted; Contacted means outreach has started; Follow-up means more action is needed; Won means the lead became a client. You can change a lead's stage from its card or profile.

Search, filters & export

Search by business or contact. Filter by stage, due date or outreach status. Export CSV downloads the current workspace lead records for reporting or backup.

Follow-up calendar

Set a follow-up date on each lead, then open Calendar to review upcoming work. Overdue and due-today filters help prioritize. Calendar dates show follow-up reminders. Automatic follow-up steps 1 and 2 are scheduled separately and must meet permission and delivery checks; their end-to-end sending has not yet been verified. Step 3 requires a manual decision.

Incoming requests

Incoming requests combines freebie requests, website contact forms and personalized demo requests. Filter by category. Remove from inbox archives a request from the CRM view but retains its original submission record. This is not permanent deletion of the original data.

Email activity & sending

Emails shows recorded outreach activity, not a guarantee of inbox delivery. Open a lead to view its recorded email history. Manual follow-up requires a documented authorized email permission status, a permission source and enabled follow-up. Preview the message and confirm the recipient before sending. Commercial campaign sending is not enabled here.

Won clients

Move a lead to Won when they become a client. The Won clients view groups these converted opportunities. It is not an invoicing or payment processor.

Your analytics

Analytics shows conversion rate (Won leads divided by total leads), leads with recorded email activity, incoming requests currently visible and overdue follow-ups. These are CRM activity metrics, not verified email deliveries, website visitor analytics or purchase revenue.

Notifications & bell

The bell stores new lead, website, freebie, demo and won-lead events. Open it to review and mark notifications as read. The app checks for updates while open. Older activity may not have a notification if it happened before notification tracking was added.

Background push on phone and tablet

Background push notifications are not active yet. Once enabled, iPhone and iPad users will need to add the web app to their Home Screen and allow notifications. Until activation, closing the CRM may prevent you from seeing new alerts immediately.

Owner notification emails

Automatic owner emails are not active yet; connection, recipient verification and delivery tests remain pending. Seeing a bell alert does not mean an email was sent. Owner operational alerts are separate from customer emails and marketing campaigns.

Privacy, permissions & security

Keep your sign-in private and only add contact information you are authorized to manage. Never mark email permission as authorized without evidence. Do not assume that a recorded sent email was delivered. Use the Privacy, Terms and Contact links on the sign-in screen for further information.

Troubleshooting

If a section looks outdated, refresh the page and sign in again. If a request is missing, check the Incoming requests category filters. If a saved change fails, read the on-screen error and avoid repeating a send action blindly. Contact support with the section name, approximate time and screenshot, without sharing passwords.
